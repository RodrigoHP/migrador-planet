# Story 18.8 — Workflow Analytics

## Story
**Como** operador que gerencia workflows AIOS,
**Quero** um comando `*workflow-analytics` que agregue metricas de todas as execucoes de workflow (state files em `.aios/`),
**Para** tomar decisoes informadas sobre performance de agentes, custo por story, taxa de falha, e tendencias.

## Status
Draft

## Context

### Problema

Existem multiplos state files em `.aios/` com dados ricos (execution_log, step_results, timestamps, retries). Mas nao ha forma de ver uma visao agregada. Nao sabemos:
- Qual agente e mais lento?
- Qual produz mais parse failures?
- O custo por story esta subindo ou descendo?
- Qual tipo de step falha mais?

### Design

#### Comando: `*workflow-analytics`

Le todos os `*-engine-state.yaml` em `.aios/` e gera report.

#### Output format

```
=== Workflow Analytics ===
Period: {oldest_run} — {newest_run} ({N} days)
Runs: {total} total ({completed} completed, {aborted} aborted)
Completion rate: {percentage}%

--- Performance ---
Avg time/workflow: {avg_duration}
Avg time/story (SDC): {avg_story_duration}
Fastest run: {id} ({duration})
Slowest run: {id} ({duration})

--- Agent Performance ---
  Agent     | Steps | Avg Time | Retry Rate | First-Try | Confidence
  @dev      |    12 |    180s  |       12%  |      88%  |      0.82
  @qa       |     8 |     90s  |        3%  |      97%  |      0.94
  @sm       |     6 |     60s  |        0%  |     100%  |      0.98
  @devops   |     4 |     45s  |        0%  |     100%  |      1.00
  @po       |     5 |     70s  |        5%  |      95%  |      0.90

--- Failure Patterns ---
  1. "{pattern}" — {occurrences}x ({percentage}% of failures)
  2. "{pattern}" — {occurrences}x
  3. "{pattern}" — {occurrences}x

--- Cost ---
Total estimated: ${total_cost}
Avg per workflow: ${avg_cost}
Avg per story: ${avg_cost_per_story}
Trend: {up/down/stable} vs previous period

--- Retry Analysis ---
Total retries: {N}
  Parse failures: {N} ({percentage}%)
  Output validation: {N} ({percentage}%)
  QA rejects: {N} ({percentage}%)
  Timeouts: {N} ({percentage}%)

--- Abort Analysis ---
Total aborts: {N}
  max_loops_exceeded: {N}

```

#### Implementacao

```
FUNCTION generate_analytics():
  state_files = glob(".aios/*-engine-state.yaml")
  states = parse_all(state_files)

  metrics = {
    total_runs: len(states),
    completed: count(s => s.status == "completed"),
    aborted: count(s => s.status == "aborted"),
    agent_metrics: aggregate_by_agent(states),
    failure_patterns: extract_patterns(states),
    cost_analysis: calculate_costs(states),
    retry_analysis: analyze_retries(states),
  }

  format_and_display(metrics)
```
